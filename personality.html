My top three traits

1. Self-motivated

One of my strongest traits is that I am self-motivated. I do not always need somebody to push me before I take action. When I have a goal, I try to find a way to accomplish it, even when the situation is difficult. I have seen financial struggles in my family, and instead of allowing that to discourage me, I use it as motivation to want something different for myself. I know that nobody can want success for me more than I want it for myself.

My recent experience working and travelling showed me this trait even more. After coming off work, I did not immediately spend everything I had earned. For months, I made a conscious effort to save and minimize unnecessary spending because I had bigger goals. There were things I wanted to buy, but I understood that sacrificing temporary wants could help me reach more important goals in the future.

2. Disciplined

Another trait I have is discipline. I believe wanting something is easy, but having the discipline to work toward it consistently is what makes the difference. Saving money for months required me to control my spending and sometimes tell myself no, even when I could afford something.

This connects strongly with the lessons in The Richest Man in Babylon. The book teaches that a person should keep a portion of everything they earn and control their expenditures rather than allowing their expenses to consume all their income.

My experience taught me that discipline is not about never enjoying life. It is about understanding when to sacrifice today so that I can have more opportunities tomorrow.

3. Independent

The third trait I would use to describe myself is independence. I have always believed that I should be willing to do things for myself. I do not want to depend on somebody else to create my future for me. If I want something, I believe I should be willing to work, learn, sacrifice, and figure out how to achieve it.

Being independent does not mean that I refuse help or advice. Instead, it means that I take responsibility for my own decisions. The Five Laws of Gold also taught me that learning from people with knowledge and experience is important, especially when dealing with money.

These three traits, self-motivation, discipline, and independence, are important to the person I am becoming. I know I still have a lot to learn, but I believe these qualities will help me achieve the financial success and personal goals I have set for myself.

My hero: myself

My hero is myself. That may sound strange to some people because most people choose a famous person, a family member, or someone they look up to as their hero. For me, however, I believe the person who has pushed me the most, especially when nobody else could, is myself.

I have seen my family go through struggles and experience failures, especially financially. Seeing those situations has influenced the way I look at life. Instead of allowing those experiences to make me give up, they have made me more determined to create a different future for myself. I know that if I want something different, I cannot simply sit back and wait for someone else to change my circumstances.

I consider myself my own hero because I keep going even when things do not work out the way I want them to. I have had moments where I could have become discouraged, but I always find a reason to try again. I am self-motivated, and I believe strongly in working for what I want. I do not expect anyone to hand me success. If I want something, I believe it is my responsibility to find a way to work toward it.

One experience that made me understand this about myself was working and travelling. After coming off work, I could have spent the money I earned without thinking about the future. Instead, for months, I made sacrifices, saved my money, and minimized my spending. There were times when I wanted to spend, but I reminded myself that I was working toward something bigger. I had to motivate myself because nobody else could make those decisions for me.

My hero is not perfect. I have made mistakes, made decisions that I could have handled differently, and experienced disappointment. However, what makes me proud of myself is that I do not allow those things to define me. I try to learn from them and keep moving forward.

A quote that represents how I feel about myself is:

“Success is not final, failure is not fatal: it is the courage to continue that counts.”
Commonly attributed to Winston Churchill

The part that means the most to me is the idea of continuing. I understand that success will not happen overnight. There will be times when I fail, lose money, face setbacks, or question whether what I am doing will work. But I believe that as long as I continue learning, working, and trying, I still have an opportunity to change my situation.

Being my own hero does not mean that I believe I know everything or that I do not need other people. I can learn from others, accept advice, and appreciate people who help me. It simply means that I understand that I am ultimately responsible for the direction of my own life.

I want to become financially successful, independent, and capable of taking care of myself and the people I care about. I want to look back one day and know that I did not allow my circumstances to determine my future.

That is why I am my own hero. I have seen difficult situations, but I am still here. I have experienced setbacks, but I am still trying. I have goals that may seem far away, but I am still working toward them. My story is not finished yet, and I believe the person who is going to change my life is ultimately me.

A tool, not a crutch

Large Language Models and Their Impact on Education and Society

Large Language Models (LLMs) are a type of artificial intelligence technology designed to process and generate human-like language. They are trained on large amounts of text and can perform tasks such as answering questions, summarizing information, translating languages, generating ideas, and producing written content. In recent years, LLMs have become increasingly visible in education, business, and everyday life.

One of the major benefits of LLMs is their ability to provide people with quick access to information and assistance. A student can use an LLM to understand a difficult topic, generate ideas for an assignment, explain unfamiliar words, or receive feedback on their writing. Research on large language models has shown that these systems can perform a wide variety of language-related tasks and have significant potential across different fields (Brown et al., 2020).

LLMs can also support personalized learning. Students do not all learn at the same speed or in the same way. An AI system can explain the same concept in different ways depending on what a student finds difficult. This can make technology useful as an additional learning resource. However, students still need to develop their own understanding instead of depending entirely on AI to provide answers.

There are also important challenges associated with LLMs. One major concern is that an LLM can produce information that sounds convincing but is incorrect. Because of this, users should verify important information using reliable sources rather than automatically assuming that an AI-generated answer is accurate. Bender et al. (2021) also discuss broader concerns surrounding large language models, including their limitations, potential harms, and the importance of considering how these systems are developed and used.

Another important issue is academic integrity. Students may be tempted to use AI to complete assignments without doing the required thinking themselves. While an LLM can be useful as a learning tool, using it to replace one’s own effort can prevent a student from developing important skills. Education is not only about producing an answer; it is also about learning how to think, research, communicate, solve problems, and develop independent judgment.

For me, the most useful way to think about LLMs is as a tool rather than a replacement for personal effort. Just as I believe in doing things for myself, I believe that technology should help me improve my abilities rather than become dependent on it. I can use AI to explain a difficult concept, help organize my thoughts, or identify areas where my writing can improve, but I should still understand the information and take responsibility for the final work.

In conclusion, Large Language Models have the potential to significantly affect education and society. They can provide useful assistance, increase access to information, and support learning. At the same time, they have limitations and raise questions about accuracy, responsible use, and academic integrity. The most important thing is how people choose to use the technology. If used responsibly, LLMs can be valuable tools for learning and personal development while still allowing students to maintain their own independence, creativity, and critical thinking.
